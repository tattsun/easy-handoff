### Restricted capability justification (`runFullTrust`)

> easy-handoff is a Win32 desktop tray app packaged with the Desktop Bridge. It needs full trust to enumerate audio endpoints with the Core Audio (MMDevice) APIs and to send connect/disconnect requests to the Bluetooth audio driver through the IKsControl kernel-streaming interface (KSPROPSETID_BtAudio), which is the same mechanism as the "Connect" button in Windows Bluetooth settings. It also shows a notification-area (tray) icon. The app does not access the network or collect any data.

## English (en-US)

### Description

Switch your Bluetooth headphones to your PC with a single click.

If you use AirPods or other Bluetooth earbuds with both your phone and your Windows PC, getting them back to the PC usually means opening Bluetooth settings and pressing "Connect" every time. easy-handoff puts that button in your system tray.

- Left-click the tray icon to connect or disconnect
- The icon shows the state at a glance: connected, disconnected, or switching
- Pick which device to control from the right-click menu (AirPods, Sony, Bose, and other Bluetooth headphones)
- Optionally launch at sign-in
- Lightweight, no network access, no data collection

The device must be paired in Windows Bluetooth settings first.

### Short description

## 日本語 (ja-JP)

### 説明

Bluetooth イヤホンを、ワンクリックで PC につなぎ直せます。

AirPods などの Bluetooth イヤホンをスマホと Windows PC の両方で使っていると、PC に戻すたびに Bluetooth 設定を開いて「接続」を押す必要があります。easy-handoff は、その「接続」ボタンをタスクトレイに置くアプリです。

- タスクトレイのアイコンを左クリックするだけで接続/切断
- アイコンで状態がひと目でわかる（接続中・切断中・切り替え中）
- 右クリックメニューで操作する機器を選べる（AirPods、Sony、Bose など）
- Windows のログイン時に自動起動も可能
- 軽量で、ネットワーク通信やデータ収集は一切なし

事前に Windows の Bluetooth 設定で機器をペアリングしておく必要があります。

### 簡単な説明
